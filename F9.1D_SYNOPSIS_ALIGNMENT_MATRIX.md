## Alignment guardrails

- Synopsis statements above are intended architecture, not evidence that a component is fully operational.
- Generic quantum modules do not establish quantum suitability, speedup, advantage, or superior recommendations.
- Do not add numeric weights or unsupported goals/risk/resource signals to force QUBO readiness.
- Do not shift the final decision away from the Executive Cognitive Brain without explicit architecture review.
