## Formulation and solver

For tasks `t` and candidate hours `h` in the validated matrix, choose binary `x[t,h]` to maximize `Σ P[t,h] x[t,h]`. Constraints: each eligible task receives exactly one hour; each candidate hour receives at most one task. No weights or additional objective terms were introduced.

The solver is a deterministic rectangular Hungarian maximum-weight assignment implementation. A separate exhaustive reference solver checks small matrices. Results include task/hour/score assignments, total objective, feasibility, constraint violations, solver name, runtime, input provenance, and production/test mode.

Existing AI recommendations, RL scheduling, legacy schedule refinement, and Executive Cognitive Brain decision roles remain in place.

They have no model/schema metadata, cannot pass the production optimizer, and require an optimizer explicitly constructed with test-matrix permission. They test assignment mechanics only and provide no evidence about personalized prediction or production non-triviality.
