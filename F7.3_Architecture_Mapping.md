## Notes
- No quantum hardware is utilized. The QAOA engine is a local simulator.
- The `ExecutiveCognitiveBrain` is present in the codebase but remains largely disconnected from the primary active runtime path (which leverages `DecisionPipeline` directly).
- The transition from Classical Optimization into Quantum Suitability and Hybrid Optimization represents the main orchestration gap that the `AIBrainController` must safely establish.
